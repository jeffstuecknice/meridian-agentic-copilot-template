# MER-POL-04 — Shipping & Delivery Policy

Northlight Electronics · Customer Care Policy Document MER-POL-04 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Shipping methods and timelines

1.1 Standard shipping (free on orders over $50): 3–5 business days.

1.2 Expedited shipping: 2 business days. **Free for Plus and Summit members** (MER-POL-02 §2); otherwise
$19.

1.3 Overnight shipping: next business day, $39. Not discounted by tier.

## §2 Same-day dispatch

2.1 In-stock items ordered before **3:00 PM local warehouse time ship the same day**.

2.2 Customer Care agents should quote the ship date, not promise a delivery date, for standard shipping.

## §3 Order confirmation and tracking

3.1 Every order receives an order reference at placement and a tracking link at dispatch.

3.2 With the customer's consent, agents may **send the receipt and tracking link by SMS** to the mobile
number on the account. Consent given verbally in the conversation is sufficient and should be noted.

## §4 Delivery issues

4.1 Lost-in-transit claims may be filed 5 business days after the last tracking update; the agent may
reship or refund per the customer's preference once filed.
