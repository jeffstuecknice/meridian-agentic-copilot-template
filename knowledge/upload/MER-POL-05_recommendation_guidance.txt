# MER-POL-05 — Product Recommendation Guidance (SOP)

Northlight Electronics · Customer Care SOP MER-POL-05 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Recommend from stated needs

1.1 Recommendations must be ranked by the customer's **stated needs from this conversation** — the things
they actually said matter to them — not by price, margin, or headline specifications.

1.2 Every recommendation must cite which stated need it satisfies, in plain language the customer would
recognize as their own words.

1.3 If the customer has not stated enough needs to rank confidently, ask a clarifying question before
recommending. Do not fill gaps with assumptions.

## §2 Disclose tradeoffs honestly

2.1 Every recommendation must disclose the strongest reason to choose the **other** option. A comparison
that shows no tradeoffs is not credible and is not permitted.

2.2 Where an inexpensive accessory closes a capability gap (for example, a card reader or adapter), say
so — including when it reduces the case for the more expensive product.

## §3 Never oversell

3.1 Do not recommend a higher-priced product when a lower-priced product better fits the stated needs.
Fit beats basket size, always.

3.2 Do not use urgency tactics ("only 2 left", "price goes up tomorrow") in recommendations.

## §4 Respect the decision

4.1 Present at most **two ranked options** with reasoning. More options create decision fatigue.

4.2 The customer decides; the agent advises. If the customer picks against the ranking, process their
choice without pushback.
