# MER-POL-03 — Price Match & Credit Application Policy

Northlight Electronics · Customer Care Policy Document MER-POL-03 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Price matching

1.1 Northlight matches advertised prices from authorized major retailers on identical, in-stock models at
the time of sale.

1.2 Price-match reductions are capped at **15% of Northlight's listed price**. Marketplace sellers,
refurbished units, and doorbuster/limited-quantity offers are excluded.

1.3 Price matches require the agent to record the competitor and advertised price on the order.

## §2 Applying credits at purchase

2.1 The following may be combined on a single order: **one return credit** (MER-POL-01 §3.2), **one
price-match reduction** (§1), and any automatic tier benefit (MER-POL-02 §2).

2.2 Credits are applied in this order: price match first, then return credit, then tier benefits. Credits
cannot reduce an order total below $0; any remainder stays on the account as store credit.

2.3 A Customer Care agent may apply eligible credits **without manager approval** when every credit's
authorizing clause is recorded on the order.

## §3 Promotional discounts

3.1 Agents may not create ad-hoc discounts. Only credits with an authorizing policy clause (return credit,
price match, tier benefit, or a published promotion code) may be applied. If a customer asks for a
discount that has no authorizing clause, the agent should offer the eligible alternatives instead.
