# MER-POL-01 — Returns & Refunds Policy

Northlight Electronics · Customer Care Policy Document MER-POL-01 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Standard return window

1.1 Merchandise may be returned for a full refund within **30 days of the delivery or in-store purchase
date** ("the standard return window").

1.2 The standard return window applies to all product categories unless a category-specific or
membership-specific exception in this policy library says otherwise. Membership-based extensions are
defined in MER-POL-02 (Northlight Circle Loyalty Program).

## §2 Condition requirements

2.1 Returned items must be in good working condition with all included accessories. Original packaging is
preferred but not required for accessories under $500.

2.2 Items showing damage from misuse are not eligible for refund and will be evaluated for warranty or
repair service instead.

## §3 Refund method and timeline

3.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** of the return being
accepted.

3.2 At the customer's request, a refund may instead be issued as **store credit, available immediately**,
which may be applied to a purchase in the same transaction (see MER-POL-03 §2 for credit application
rules).

## §4 Where returns can be processed

4.1 Returns may be processed in-store, by mail, or **by a Customer Care agent during a support
conversation** when the purchase is verifiable on the customer's account.

4.2 A Customer Care agent processing a return must record the originating order reference and the policy
clause that authorizes the return.

## §5 Denied returns and escalation

5.1 If a return is denied at one channel (for example, in-store), the customer may request re-evaluation
through Customer Care. **A prior denial does not bind a later evaluation** if the first evaluation
applied the wrong policy terms — for example, if a membership benefit in MER-POL-02 was not taken into
account.
