# MER-POL-02 — Northlight Circle Loyalty Program

Northlight Electronics · Customer Care Policy Document MER-POL-02 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Program tiers

1.1 Northlight Circle has three tiers: **Member** (free enrollment), **Plus** (earned at $1,500 annual
spend), and **Summit** (earned at $4,000 annual spend or 3+ years of Plus membership).

1.2 Tier status is visible on the customer's account record and applies automatically — customers do not
need to claim tier benefits for them to be honored.

## §2 Benefits summary

| Benefit | Member | Plus | Summit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Return window (all products) | 30 days | 30 days | 30 days |
| Return window (accessories) | 30 days | 40 days | **45 days** |
| Free expedited shipping | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Dedicated Care line | — | — | ✓ |
| Early access to launches | — | ✓ | ✓ |

## §3 Extended accessory return window

3.1 **Summit members may return accessories within 45 days of the delivery or purchase date.** Plus
members may return accessories within 40 days. "Accessories" includes docking stations, cables, hubs,
keyboards, mice, cases, and chargers.

3.2 The extended window in §3.1 is a **membership entitlement, not a courtesy exception** — it requires no
manager approval and should be applied automatically whenever the customer's tier qualifies.

3.3 If a return inside the extended window was previously denied under the standard 30-day terms, the
denial should be corrected per MER-POL-01 §5.1 and the return processed with a note citing this clause.

## §4 Combining refunds and credits with new purchases

4.1 A refund issued as store credit (MER-POL-01 §3.2) may be **applied immediately to a new purchase in
the same conversation**, including purchases assisted by Customer Care.

4.2 There is no minimum purchase amount for applying a return credit. Credits apply to the pre-tax total.

## §5 Tier protection

5.1 Processing a return does not reduce a customer's tier standing for the current membership year.
