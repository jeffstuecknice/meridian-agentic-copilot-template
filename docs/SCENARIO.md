## The two laptops (product catalog — mock API data, NOT Knowledge Store)

| | **Aero 14** | **Titan 16** |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $1,299 | $1,899 |
| Weight | 2.7 lb | 4.9 lb |
| Battery | 18 hr | 9 hr |
| Display | 14" 2.8K, 500 nits | 16" 4K, 600 nits, wide-gamut |
| Performance | Great for photo editing, light video | Workstation-class, heavy video/3D |
| Ports | 2× USB-C | 2× USB-C, HDMI, SD slot |
| In stock | Yes — ships today | Yes — ships today |

**The tension:** on raw specs the Titan 16 "wins" — but Maya's *stated needs* (works from cafés, travels
twice a month, battery anxiety, mostly photo editing not video) rank the **Aero 14** first. The AI must
recommend from what she *said*, and show its reasoning per card ("you said battery matters most → 18 hr
vs 9 hr"). The SD-card slot is the one genuine Titan pull for a photographer — the AI should surface it
honestly (and note the $29 USB-C SD reader closes the gap). Honest tradeoffs, not a rigged comparison.

## The sore point (return subplot → sentiment dip, HEARD moment, policy exception)

Maya bought a **ProDock USB-C docking station ($189)** 34 days ago. She tried to return it in-store last
week and was told she's outside the **30-day standard return window**. She's annoyed — she mentions it
mid-conversation and her sentiment drops.

**The policy fact (Knowledge Store, MER-POL-02):** Northlight Circle **Summit** members get a **45-day
return window** on accessories. The store associate applied the standard policy by mistake. The AI finds
the clause, quotes it verbatim on the card, and proposes: process the return exception, apply the $189 as
credit toward the new laptop.

## Approvable actions (Execute-flow tool registry)

Generic + parameterized, per the playbook — one registry serves many domains. An approved beat's
`exec:{actions:[…]}` runs them all in ONE deterministic `execute_batch` call; the Command Agent calls
them individually.

| Tool | Parameters (all `type:string`) | Demo use |
|---|---|---|
| `process_return_exception` | orderRef, item, amount, clause, reason | ProDock return under MER-POL-02 §3.1 |
| `apply_credit` | amount, unit, reason | $189 credit to the order |
| `place_order` | sku, creditApplied, shipMethod | Aero 14 at $1,110 after credit, ships today |
| `send_receipt` | name, detail (`title — REF\|title — REF`), total, orderRef | Receipt page + short link the agent delivers **in the chat** |
| `escalate_case` | summary, queue | The honest fallback for anything beyond policy |

Every execution returns a **real confirmation reference** (e.g. `RMA-88214`, `CR-10387`, `ORD-55901`)
generated by the mock API — never invented by the LLM. (Standing rule: real data or a visible error.)
On SMS: this project has no SMS provider connection yet, so the receipt is delivered as a short link
in the live chat — genuinely delivered, never a pretend-SMS (see `api/README.md`).

## The seven beats — transcript skeleton

Customer lines are what the tester pastes (they trigger the flow). Agent lines reach the flow via
`includeAgentUtterances` or tile postbacks.

**Beat 1 — accept + pre-load.** Riley accepts. Tile: skeleton → "AI Agent is reviewing the conversation…"
→ Maya's profile card (Summit badge, 3 yr, recent orders incl. the ProDock).

**Beat 2 — listen.**
> **Maya:** Hi! I'm trying to pick a new laptop and I'm stuck between the Aero 14 and the Titan 16. I'm a
> photographer — I mostly edit photos, some short video. I work out of cafés most days and fly about twice
> a month, so battery life is a big deal. Honestly the dead battery on my old machine is why I'm here.

*Look for:* needs chips appear (battery ★, portability ★, photo editing, occasional video).

**Beat 3 — propose (showpiece).** Comparison cards render: **Aero 14 ranked #1** with per-need reasoning
tied to her words; Titan 16 card honest ("wins on raw power and the SD slot — but 9 hr battery vs your
stated dealbreaker"). Tell line for Riley to say.

**Beat 4 — sentiment dip + HEARD.**
> **Maya:** Before I buy anything though — I'm still annoyed about that docking station I tried to return
> last week. The store said I was past the window. I've been a member for three years, that felt bad.

*Look for:* sentiment slider drops (amber/rose). Riley acknowledges ("I hear you — let me look at that
right now, that's not the experience we want for you"). *Look for:* green "AI Agent heard this" banner,
slider recovers.

**Beat 5 — policy exception.** Card appears: "Return eligible under Northlight Circle policy" with the
**verbatim MER-POL-02 §3 quote**, proposing: process return + apply $189 credit toward the Aero 14
($1,299 → $1,110).

**Beat 6 — approve → execute.** Riley clicks **Approve** on the bundle card (`SDK.postback`). Execute flow
runs: `RMA-…`, `CR-…`, `ORD-…` refs animate onto the card in ~4-8 s.

**Beat 7 — wrap.**
> **Maya:** That's amazing, thank you! Can you send me the receipt?

Riley approves the receipt beat → `send_receipt` builds the receipt page + short link → Riley pastes
the link into the chat (genuinely delivered over the live channel). Optional along the way: Maya asks
"does the Aero 14 support two external monitors?" → KB answer card → **Add to steps**.
